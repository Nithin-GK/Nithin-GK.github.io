I develop long-horizon agents that interpret human interactions and build the simulations we use to evaluate them.

During my Ph.D. at Johns Hopkins University, I worked with [Vishal M. Patel](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=AkEXTbIAAAAJ&hl=en) on controlling diffusion models and using synthetic data to help vision models generalize.

<details class="intro-background" markdown="1">
<summary>More about my research</summary>

My research spans large-scale model training, generative modeling, multimodal learning, and 3D understanding. I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the VIU Lab. Before Hopkins, I completed a dual B.Tech. and M.Tech. in Electrical Engineering at IIT Madras, where I studied image reconstruction with [A. N. Rajagopalan](https://scholar.google.co.in/citations?user=jXTZEw4AAAAJ&hl=en) at the IPCV Lab.

I find the connection between diffusion models and the physics of Brownian motion fascinating. I’ve worked on image, video, and 3D generation. I welcome collaborations in generative modeling.
</details>
