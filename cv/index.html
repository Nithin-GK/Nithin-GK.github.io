Research Highlights

Inference-Time Steering and Compositional Generation

2022 – 2024

  • Introduced Steered Diffusion, the first plug-and-play framework to unify image-to-image and label-conditioned generation through inference-time steering, without task-specific training (ICCV 2023).
  • Pioneered compositional multimodal generation with Unite and Conquer, combining independently trained diffusion models at inference time without joint multimodal training (CVPR 2023).
  • Extended pretrained text-to-image models to multiple simultaneous conditions through variance-guided feature fusion (MaxFusion, ECCV 2024).

Efficient Diffusion Steering (D3SG)

2026

  • Developed D3SG for guidance without U-Net backpropagation, combining noise-space and image-space gradients with adaptive scaling for linear and nonlinear conditions. Research manuscript.

Synthetic Data for Generalizable Image Restoration (GenDeg)

2025

  • Introduced GenDeg to synthesize realistic image degradations from clean inputs, improving all-in-one restoration on unseen degradation patterns (CVPR 2025).
  • Built GenDS (750K+ samples), including 550K+ synthetic image pairs spanning six degradation types.

IARPA BRIAR – Unconstrained Face Recognition

2021 – 2025

  • Achieved state-of-the-art results on five face-recognition benchmarks through large-scale pretraining, staged fine-tuning, and context-aware face crops (FG 2025; co-first author).
  • Surpassed AdaFace by 31.4 percentage points in BRIAR TAR at FAR 10−3, with both models pretrained on WebFace12M; achieved 81.38% Rank-1 with WebFace42M.
