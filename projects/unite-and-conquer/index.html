Independent models. One composed generation.

The question

The challenge.

A model trained for one condition does not automatically understand another. Joint multimodal training needs paired data across the conditions and repeated training for new combinations.

The approach

The central idea.

Compose independently trained conditional diffusion models at inference time. Reliability weights balance their shared unconditional estimate, while guidance strengths control the individual conditions.

Behind the result

How it works.

  1. 01

    Select diffusion models trained for the individual conditions, such as an object class and a text description.

  2. 02

    Combine their conditional and null-condition predictions using the product-of-experts formulation and reliability weights.

  3. 03

    Sample one image with the combined guidance so the result can express the conditions together.

Composing independent diffusion models

Unite and Conquer combines independently trained, modality-conditioned diffusion models into a single sampling process. A generalized product of experts provides a way to combine their information without jointly training a new model on every modality combination.

Each model contributes a conditional prediction. Reliability-weighted predictions with a null condition estimate the shared unconditional density used in the composition.

Research contributions

  • Pioneered compositional multimodal generation by combining independent diffusion models through a generalized product of experts.
  • Introduced controllable modality weights to balance the influence of different models.
  • Demonstrated joint class-and-text constraints without training a new model for every modality combination.
