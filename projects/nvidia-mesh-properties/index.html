NVIDIA 2024 · Research draft

Physical-property assignment to 3D meshes

Use visual foundation models and multimodal reasoning to divide 3D objects into meaningful parts and estimate what those parts are made of.

Research intern

How it works

From rendered views to parts

Foundation-model features and region masks from multiple rendered views are refined into a consistent 3D feature field. Clustering and multimodal reasoning produce part groups and estimated material labels.

Research and invention

This work is documented in the SmartTagger research draft. I am a co-inventor on U.S. Patent Application 19/024,394, published as US20260038283A1 on February 5, 2026. The mass-estimation benchmark is reported in the draft, separately from the patent application.
