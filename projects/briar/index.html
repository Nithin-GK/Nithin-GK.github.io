Unconstrained face recognition

Train and adapt face-recognition models for images that are distant, small or degraded, where clean close-up training alone is not enough.

Co-first author

How it works

Large-scale pretraining and adaptation

A transformer learns face representations from large aligned datasets, then adapts to low-quality imagery through linear probing and full-model fine-tuning. Enlarged aligned crops preserve context around the face.

Separate pretraining settings

The BRIAR reported comparison uses WebFace12M with different architectures: AdaFace ResNet101 and Swin-B. A separate Swin-B model pretrained on WebFace42M reaches 81.38% Rank-1. The paper also evaluates TinyFace, IJB-S, IJB-B and IJB-C.

Results

The system accepts more genuine face matches while holding the false-accept rate at 0.1%. Both compared models use WebFace12M pretraining, but their architectures differ.
