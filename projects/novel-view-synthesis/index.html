Novel view synthesis at scale

Training large vision transformers to create new views of a scene, using generated examples to expand the available training data.

Co-first author

How it works

Training with generated views

CAT3D generates additional camera-conditioned observations. Tok-D separates source-image tokens from target-view tokens through layer-wise modulation, reducing the effect of artifacts in generated views on reconstruction.

Training and evaluation

The paper reports training on eight H100 GPUs for 100,000 iterations. Its real-data architecture comparison and synthetic-augmentation experiment are separate: the +1.64 dB DL3DV result is from the real-data comparison, while generated views produce smaller cross-dataset gains.
