Scale-Wise VAR

Show how a particular scale-by-scale image generator can behave like a diffusion model, allowing it to refine its predictions.

Co-first author

How it worksResultsPresentationPaper ↗

How it works

  1. Express next-scale prediction as a transition between discrete visual-token representations.

  2. Apply the Markovian attention mask and relate the resulting transitions and training objective to discrete diffusion.

  3. Use this interpretation in Scalable Visual Refinement with Discrete Diffusion (SRDD) to bring iterative refinement into the scale-wise generation framework.

Scale-wise generation as discrete diffusion

Scale-wise VAR generates visual tokens from coarse scales to finer scales. This work shows that, under a Markovian attention mask, its generation process is mathematically equivalent to a discrete diffusion process.

The attention-mask condition is central to the equivalence. It provides a principled way to connect autoregressive token prediction with diffusion-style refinement.

Results

The comparison uses a matched 200-epoch training study across four benchmarks. Lower FID indicates closer agreement between generated and reference image distributions.
