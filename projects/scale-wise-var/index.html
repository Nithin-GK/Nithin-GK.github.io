The question

The challenge.

Scale-wise autoregressive generation attends to a history of earlier scales. That history can introduce redundancy, while diffusion methods offer useful ways to refine an imperfect prediction.

The approach

The central idea.

Apply a Markovian attention mask that conditions each scale on its immediate predecessor. The resulting discrete-diffusion interpretation motivates resampling and refinement.

Measured impact

The results.

Refinement improves the reported generation-quality score across four benchmarks. Lower FID means the generated image distribution is closer to the reference dataset.

Behind the result

How it works.

  1. 01

    Express next-scale prediction as a transition between discrete visual-token representations.

  2. 02

    Apply the Markovian attention mask and relate the resulting transitions and training objective to discrete diffusion.

  3. 03

    Use this interpretation in Scalable Visual Refinement with Discrete Diffusion (SRDD) to bring iterative refinement into the scale-wise generation framework.

Scale-wise generation as discrete diffusion

Scale-wise VAR generates visual tokens from coarse scales to finer scales. This work shows that, under a Markovian attention mask, its generation process is mathematically equivalent to a discrete diffusion process.

The attention-mask condition is central to the equivalence. It provides a principled way to connect autoregressive token prediction with diffusion-style refinement.

Research contributions

  • Established an equivalence between scale-wise VAR and discrete diffusion under a Markovian attention mask.
  • Used the connection to develop Scalable Visual Refinement with Discrete Diffusion (SRDD).
  • Co-first author with Amandeep Kumar.
